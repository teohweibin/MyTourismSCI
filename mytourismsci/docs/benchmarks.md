# MyTourismSCI Benchmarks — Reference Document

Three distinct benchmarks anchor MyTourismSCI's methodology, positioning, 
and validation. Every teammate should cite the appropriate benchmark(s) 
when writing report sections.

## Benchmark 1: Methodological Authority (Section 3 of report)

Every construction choice in MyTourismSCI traces to established composite-
index methodology:

- OECD & JRC (2008), *Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators: 
  Methodology and User Guide.* — Primary reference for the 10-step 
  construction process
- UNDP Human Development Report Office. Technical Notes (2010 and current). 
  — Justifies geometric mean aggregation
- Alkire, S. & Foster, J. (2011). Counting and multidimensional poverty 
  measurement. Journal of Public Economics. — Justifies sub-national 
  composite index approach; adopted by DOSM as Malaysian MPI methodology
- UN Tourism, Measuring the Sustainability of Tourism (MST) Framework. 
  — Justifies the pillar structure (Economic, Environmental, Social)
- UNESCAP (2020), Study on Measuring the Sustainable Tourism (MST) in 
  Malaysia. — Malaysian implementation reference for pillar scope

## Benchmark 2: Novelty and Positioning (Sections 1, 2, 7 of report)

MyTourismSCI's positioning against Malaysia's current tourism measurement 
landscape:

Current landscape (which MyTourismSCI complements, not replaces):
- DOSM Domestic Tourism Survey — state-level visitor volume, expenditure, 
  demographics
- DOSM Tourism Satellite Account — national-level tourism GDP, 
  employment, macroeconomic footprint (Sabah RTSA is the only sub-national 
  TSA)
- DOSM SDG Indicators publication — national-level SDG fragments 
  including SDG 8.9.1 and 8.9.2
- DOSM Beyond GDP and Malaysian MPI — multidimensional composite 
  instruments (not tourism-specific but methodological precedent)
- MOTAC and Tourism Malaysia — volume, revenue, hotel occupancy statistics

MyTourismSCI's specific position:
- Extends the current landscape by synthesizing existing measurement 
  dimensions into a state-comparable composite instrument
- Operationalizes Malaysia's UN MST framework commitment and RMK13 
  Strategy A1.10 tourism sustainability mandate at sub-national 
  granularity
- Applies DOSM's own composite-index methodological practice (from 
  Beyond GDP and Malaysian MPI) to a tourism-specific domain not 
  currently covered

## Benchmark 3: External Validation (Section 4.4 and 6.2 of report)

MyTourismSCI's construct validity is checked against two references:

- WEF Travel & Tourism Development Index (T&TDI), most recent edition 
  (~2024). — Country-level tourism instrument; national MyTourismSCI 
  aggregate trajectory compared to Malaysia's T&TDI trajectory 2020–2025 
  for directional correspondence
- Face-validity case narratives for 3 states (high, mid, low scoring) 
  — Qualitative documented tourism reality checked against MyTourismSCI 
  quantitative ranking

## Language Convention When Discussing Benchmarks

Every mention of DOSM's current instruments must use extension/
complement language, not gap/failure language:
- Never write: "DOSM does not measure tourism sustainability"
- Always write: "DOSM's current instrument set does not yet include a 
  state-comparable tourism sustainability composite"

MyTourismSCI is designed to support DOSM's operationalization of 
existing commitments (UN MST, RMK13), not to fill gaps DOSM has left.
